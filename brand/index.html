Brand identity kit

The mark, the map,
the mandate.

Everything Wildpath puts into the world, a wordmark, a photograph, a sentence, a lodge wall, answers to four words: travel the untamed beauty. This page is the system that holds those words accountable.

01

Primary
Wordmark

The Wildpath logo is not an icon. It is not a badge, a shield, or an emblem. It is a typographic wordmark, two words, one line, generous breathing room. WILDPATH dominates; ADVENTURES sits to its right, vertically centred, never below, never stacked.

WILDPATHADVENTURES

Travel the untamed beauty

02, Clear Space

The wordmark requires whitespace equal to the height of the letter W on all sides. Nothing may intrude on this margin, not text, not images, not borders.

WILDPATHADVENTURES

Minimum clear space = height of W

03, Minimum Scale

The wordmark must never appear smaller than 24px on screen. Below this threshold, ADVENTURES becomes illegible and the hierarchy collapses.

06

Typography
System

Three voices. One register. Anton carries the wordmark and headlines, ultra-condensed, bold, uppercase. Archivo handles section titles and labels. Caveat appears only for the tagline, quotes, and section introductions.

Primary, Anton

Untamed

Secondary, Archivo

beauty

07

Voice

Four rules the
copy obeys.

01

Locate, do not engineer

We do not manufacture experiences. We find them, then clear the path. The land is the author. We are the editors.

02

Sensory over superlative

Write what the boot feels, what the air smells like at dawn, what the silence sounds like. Avoid adjectives that could describe any hotel anywhere.

03

Temporary presence, permanent impact

Our guests leave. The lodges remain. The land remains. Every sentence should honour that asymmetry.

04

No slop

Forbidden words: revolutionize, unlock, seamless, next-generation, cutting-edge, boutique, curated, bespoke. If a travel brochure uses it, we do not.

09

Brand values

What we
stand on.

Namibian Roots

Every guide, every lodge, every route is Namibian. We are not headquartered in London or New York.

Authentic Expeditions

We move on foot, by Cessna, and by Land Cruiser. Never by coach, never by script.

Responsible Tourism

Our footprint is measured against the land, not against a balance sheet. Carbon logged, not offset.

Conservation

A percentage of every journey funds the Save the Rhino Trust and the Namibian Desert Lion Project.

Local Communities

We hire from Damaraland, from the Kunene, from Windhoek. Our supply chain is 90% Namibian.

Slow Travel

We do not do 14 parks in 7 days. We do one landscape deeply, then move.

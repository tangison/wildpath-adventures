Victoria Falls

Mosi-oa-Tunya, the smoke that thunders. One of the largest sheets of falling water in the world.

The Kololo people called it Mosi-oa-Tunya, “the smoke that thunders”, and the spray is visible from a great distance. The falls mark the border between Zimbabwe and Zambia, and a natural endpoint or extension to any southern African journey.

Best time

February to May, peak flow. September to October, low flow, less spray, clearer views.

Wildlife

  • Elephant (in town itself)
  • Hippo
  • Crocodile
  • Birdlife

The land itself

Basalt gorge, rainforest from the spray, the Zambezi above and below.

Why it stays with you.

  • —Falls walk, full length
  • —Helicopter flight
  • —Zambezi sunset cruise
  • —Victoria Falls Bridge
